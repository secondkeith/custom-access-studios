# Design direction

## Positioning
A guide between a person's needs and the products and people that can help. Not a construction firm, medical provider, product certification body, or online shop.

## Visual language
Editorial residential design; warm paper (#f5f2e9), deep green ink (#243c3b), muted sage (#dce2d5), rust (#a55032), cream (#e9deca). Instrument Serif for expressive large headings, DM Sans for readable explanation and controls. Locally hosted fonts and assets; no runtime third-party requests.

Doorway/connection motif in the wordmark, favicon and image crop. Alternating editorial columns, paired service paths, native expandable examples, an ordered process, and a dark manifesto panel. Avoid medical stock imagery, paternalistic language, simulated technical drawings, false project imagery, and excessive decorative motion.

## UX
Single narrative homepage rather than multiple thin service pages. Two public pages: the homepage and accessibility/privacy page. Image direction is internal production material excluded from GitHub Pages. Large native interactive elements, visible focus, skip links, reduced-motion support, responsive reflow. No autoplay or reveal-on-scroll.

The first-thought notepad is a deliberate preview affordance, not a substitute for a launch inquiry route. It does not transmit data. Public contact details and the service offering must be agreed before launch.

## Self-audit
Initial design does not use tech gradients, default indigo, three equal icon cards, accent rails, glass blur, invented statistics, icon toppers, or center-stacked sections. Type is deliberately chosen; primary surface is Decide/Learn. However, warm serif + arched image is an established boutique-design idiom rather than a wholly original identity. Independent critique is required; passing an anti-slop checklist does not establish award-level quality.

## Imagery
Hero reference is provisional. Final imagery should show real everyday usability and human agency. No generated work may be represented as a completed project. See `_internal/image-briefs.html` for internal Gemini prompts, crops, resolutions, and guardrails; this brief is not a public page.
