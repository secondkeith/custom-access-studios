A closer look at reach · An illustrative conversation

What stays.
What could change.

The mug matters. Not just the cupboard.

In a conversation about reach, the starting point could be making tea: which part is difficult, which favourite things should stay, and what a comfortable change would mean to you.

From that conversation to a useful handoff

Emily’s intended role is to help turn those priorities into questions for a supplier or specialist—not to choose a fitting without an assessment.

An illustrative handoff: a note about the routine, the things to preserve, and questions about fit, materials, cost, repairability, and installation. A supplier could explain a product’s limits; a cabinetmaker could assess whether an alteration is suitable.

This is a proposed way of working, not a completed client story or a confirmed service package.

A guide, not every specialist.

Emily’s planned role is to clarify options and connect people. Clinical advice, technical assessment, design, and installation belong with the appropriately qualified professionals. Services and scope are still being developed.
