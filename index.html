Explore the thinking together
01

Understand the difficulty

Is the shelf too high, the handle hard to grip, or the mug heavy when full? Which part of making tea takes the most effort?

02

Look beyond the mug

What about lifting the kettle, carrying your drink or reaching other everyday things? You can tell us whether any of these feel awkward too.

03

Consider what fits your life

A lower shelf, a different cup or a ready-made fitting may help. If not, custom storage could be explored with a suitable specialist—with your favourite mug still part of the plan.

04

Revisit as things change

Does it still feel comfortable when you’re tired, or if you use the kitchen differently? A useful change today can be reconsidered later, with you.

Thoughtful choices, not more promises

Look past
the promise.

Existing or custom, an option should earn its place in your life. Practical support and personal style belong together—pieces you enjoy living with, whether you’re on your own or welcoming friends. CAS would help you ask:

Does it fit?
Your routine, space, comfort and budget—not just a measurement on a box.
Will it hold up?
Materials, construction, upkeep, repairability and the details of a warranty.
What backs the claim?
Clear specifications, credible evidence and honest limits. If an answer is missing, it’s a question to pursue.

This is how we hope to help you explore your options. It does not certify a product or guarantee it will suit you. Where needed, clinical advice, technical checks, making custom pieces and installation require appropriately qualified professionals.

A relationship, not a one-time answer

Still you.

same needs.

A change in energy. A different home. A new way of getting around. Or simply a solution that no longer feels right.

Our intention is to be a partner you can return to: revisit what’s working, notice related difficulties, and consider what might help next. You set the pace—and decide how far ahead you want to look.
