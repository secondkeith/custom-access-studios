02 / Two paths. Your starting point.

Find what fits.
Or find who can make it.

You don’t have to choose a path before we talk. A home may need a little of both.

Already out there01

Ready-made.
Thoughtfully chosen.

The right product can make a daily task easier. Finding it—and knowing if it’s well made, suitable, and straightforward to use—is the harder part.

Emily helps narrow the options, ask the useful questions, and connect you with suppliers and installation support.

  • Can you comfortably reach, grip, and use it?
  • What do the materials, warranty, and repair options tell us?
  • What will installation and upkeep actually involve?

03 / Think in everyday moments

What would you like
to make easier?

Start with the task, not the technology. These are possibilities to explore—not prescriptions or completed projects.

01Reaching what you use every day

Making breakfast shouldn’t start with a struggle to reach a shelf.

We might explore better-organized storage, pull-down fittings, or a conversation with a cabinetmaker about a solution for your space. The right answer depends on your reach, the room, and what you want to keep close.

Possible paths: ready-made + custom
02Making light easier to control

A switch should work for you, not the other way around.

Simple remotes, thoughtfully placed controls, or compatible lighting technology may help. We’d consider what feels comfortable to use, what happens if the internet goes down, and whether an installer is needed.

Possible path: ready-made
03Moving between familiar spaces

The doorway you use every day deserves a closer look.

A threshold, door fitting, or narrow route can change how a room feels to use. Emily can help identify the questions and connect you with a qualified professional to assess suitable changes.

Possible paths: ready-made + custom
