room for you.

This website is an early preview. We’re working to make it accessible, and further testing is still needed.

Using this website

  • Use “Skip to content” to move past the navigation.
  • Use your keyboard to follow links, explore the examples and save your note.
  • A visible outline shows which link or control you have selected.
  • You can enlarge text with your browser’s controls, and the layout adjusts to your screen.
  • Nothing plays automatically. If your device is set to reduce motion, the page won’t use smooth scrolling.
  • Images have written descriptions, and you do not need to hover over anything to reveal information.

Accessibility: work still to do

We’re working toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA, an accessibility standard for websites. We have not yet confirmed that the site meets it.

Further checks with screen readers and feedback from people with different access needs are still needed. Your downloaded note is a text file. On a phone or tablet, it may open first so you can choose where to save it.

Your note and your privacy

Use the notepad on the home page to collect your thoughts. You can change or remove anything, review your note, then copy, download or print it. Adding an example to an existing note asks your permission before replacing your writing. Typing does not send your note to CAS or save it on a server. You do not need to include names or medical details. Your browser may remember what you typed through its own autofill or page-restore features, but this website does not save it for your next visit.

Choosing Open email draft passes your complete note to your email app. The app may save or sync drafts under its own privacy policy. You’ll need an email app set up on your device. This website does not send the email, and cannot tell whether the draft opened or the message was delivered. Review the draft before choosing whether to send it. If your note is too long to open as a draft, we ask you to copy it instead—we never shorten your writing. If the draft does not open, choose Copy text and paste your note into an email. If copying does not work, download the text or select and copy the preview. The note tools need JavaScript; if it is unavailable, use your own notes app instead.

This site has no analytics or advertising trackers. GitHub Pages serves the site and may process technical information such as IP addresses in accordance with GitHub’s privacy statement.

Service availability and feedback

Services and service inquiries are not open yet. The email draft uses info@customaccessstudios.com. Delivery and replies at this email address have not yet been confirmed. If you received this preview from someone, you can send feedback to that person.

The everyday examples are imagined situations, not completed client projects or recommendations for your home. They do not guarantee an outcome. What suits you depends on your needs and your home. Qualified specialists should assess any proposed home alterations.
