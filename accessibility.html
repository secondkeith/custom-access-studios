Access is part of the work

A site that makes
room for you.

This is an early concept for Custom Access Studios. Accessibility is part of how we are building it—not a claim that the work is finished.

What’s built in

  • A skip link to get past the navigation.
  • Keyboard-operable links, everyday examples, and note-saving controls.
  • Visible keyboard focus, descriptive headings, and labeled text fields.
  • Responsive layouts and text that can be enlarged with browser controls.
  • No autoplaying media. Reduced-motion preferences disable smooth scrolling.
  • Written image descriptions and no information available only through hover.

What we’re working toward

Our target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This is not a certification or a declaration of full compliance. Automated checks cannot replace testing with people, assistive technology, and real devices.

The full site still needs a manual screen-reader review and feedback from people with different access needs. The downloadable note is plain text; mobile browsers may open it rather than save it automatically.

Your starting note: local typing, optional sharing

The notepad on the home page builds a conversation sheet in your browser. You can edit or remove every field, review the sheet, then copy its text, download a text file, or print it. Importing an example into an existing note requires confirmation before replacing your writing. Typing does not upload your note or submit a form, and this site does not store the text on a server. No names or medical details are required. Your browser may keep form values through its own restore or autofill features; the website does not use local storage to retain them.

Choosing Open email draft explicitly shares all four fields with your configured mail app via an email link. That app may sync or store drafts under its own privacy policies. A configured mail app is required; the website does not send the email. Review the complete draft and choose whether to send it yourself. We cannot confirm that a draft opened or that a message was delivered. Email links have varying length limits; this site blocks links over 1,800 encoded characters rather than trim your text. Use Copy text and paste the full note into your mail app if the note is too long or the link fails. If copying fails, download the text or select and copy the preview. Without JavaScript, these controls remain disabled; use your own notes app instead.

This site has no analytics or advertising trackers. GitHub Pages serves the site and may process technical information such as IP addresses in accordance with GitHub’s privacy statement.

Preview limitations & feedback

Services and service inquiries are not open yet. The email draft uses info@customaccessstudios.com. Preview contact: delivery and replies at this address have not yet been confirmed. If you received this preview from someone, you can send feedback to that person.

The everyday examples describe possibilities, not completed projects, individual recommendations, or guarantees. Product suitability depends on the person, the home, and appropriate assessment. Any home alterations need appropriate assessment by qualified specialists.
